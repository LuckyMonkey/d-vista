# Runbook: N1 Capture (Dalí–Edison)

Protocol ID: D-VISTA-RB-N1
Status: **Optional / experimental**
Purpose: capture hypnagogic imagery or associations near sleep onset. **This is not the main lucid-dream induction procedure.**

## What this is

Salvador Dalí described a **key falling onto a plate**; Thomas Edison is associated with **metal balls dropping** as the hand relaxes. The falling object wakes the resting person near the threshold of sleep so they can recall an idea. The historical anecdote is not proof of effectiveness at lucid-dream induction.

**N1** (non-REM stage 1) is a transitional sleep stage associated with hypnagogic images, fragments of speech, and drifting associations. Lacaux et al. (2021) found a task-specific benefit for creative insight after brief N1 sleep. That experiment used sleep monitoring to characterize sleep stages; a dropped object **cannot identify N1 precisely**.

D-VISTA uses N1 Capture as an **idea-retrieval experiment**. LCP remains a separate **dream-recognition experiment**, most often attempted during ordinary nocturnal dreams.

## Preferred procedure: natural capture (no prop)

1. Choose a short, low-stakes **daytime rest** period when you are comfortably rested and safe to doze. Do not shorten nighttime sleep.
2. Place a notebook or voice recorder within easy reach and choose a single question or theme (for example, **“What would it feel like to have Wi-Fi without a device?”**).
3. Sit comfortably in a stable, supported seat, or lie down normally. Relax. Don't force yourself to remain conscious.
4. If you drift toward sleep and **wake naturally**, write down the first fragment you remember—image, phrase, sensation, or association.
5. Stop after one short attempt if nothing happens. Don't chase the effect by repeatedly fragmenting sleep.
6. Mark the log **N1-UNVERIFIED** unless formal sleep staging was measured. Record *possible hypnagogic experience*, not “confirmed N1.”

## Historical prop demonstration (optional, not needed)

The original key/ball trick can be demonstrated without turning it into a bedside hazard:

- If trying it at all, do so **only while seated securely during the day**, fully supported, with a **small, lightweight, non-sharp object** held over a **padded, stable catch area** close to the floor.
- Use no glass, sharp edges, heavy metal objects, electrical components, or devices held over your feet or body.
- The object may fall when your hand relaxes; if the sound or motion wakes you, note any remembered imagery.
- If the setup is awkward or might cause injury, skip it and use **natural capture** instead.
- Don't do this in bed overnight, while standing, while responsible for another person, or in any setting in which suddenly dozing or startling awake would be unsafe.

There is **no requirement** for an audible metallic clang, and no evidence that a louder impact improves results.

## Integration with dWLAN

You can prepare the same *semantic cue* used by the primary D-VISTA protocol:

**“I have Wi-Fi.”**

During a brief waking pre-rest rehearsal, imagine knowing Wi-Fi exists in a place where you have no device. If a dreamlike fragment later involves Wi-Fi, log **dream-theme incorporation**. Do **not** count it as lucidity unless you actually knew you were dreaming *while the experience continued*.

The desired N1 outcome is **retrieval of a fragment**. The desired overnight LCP outcome is **recognition of dreaming**. Do not merge the metrics.

## Capture record

~~~text
D-VISTA N1 CAPTURE
Date/time:
Method: NATURAL / LIGHT PROP (optional)
Sleep stage: UNVERIFIED (unless measured)
Theme:
First remembered fragment (verbatim):
Wi-Fi concept present: YES / NO / UNSURE
Insight or association (interpretation, separate from fragment):
Sleep quality afterward: BETTER / SAME / WORSE
Distress or startle: NONE / MILD / SIGNIFICANT
~~~

A compelling idea is worth investigating while awake; it isn't automatically correct because it arose near sleep.

## Stop conditions

Stop if this makes you more tired, repeatedly wakes you, provokes frightening experiences, worsens nightmares or insomnia, or creates confusion after waking. Leave glucose monitors, medication routines, and any medical alerts exactly as prescribed. **Never** sacrifice a needed rest period to chase the N1 boundary.

## Research and limitations

- [Lacaux et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj5866): short N1 and one hidden-rule insight task, **not lucidity**.
- [Horowitz et al., 2020, Dormio](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2020.102938): sound-cued **dream incubation at sleep onset**, involving monitoring and repeated prompts.
- [Horowitz et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-31361-w): targeted incubation and **theme-related creative performance**.
- [MIT Dormio overview](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/sleep-creativity/overview/): Edison/Dalí historical background.
- [Evidence register](../docs/evidence-register.md): separates creative insight, incorporation, and lucidity.

**Acceptance criterion:** a dated, accurately labeled fragment or an honest “nothing recalled.” Neither implies a lucid dream.
