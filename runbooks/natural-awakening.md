# Runbook: Natural Awakening

```text
Runbook:    dDNS-RB-NATURAL-AWAKENING
Duration:   1-3 minutes, then sleep
Trigger:    you woke up during the night on your own
Optional:   yes, entirely
```

People often wake briefly during the night, especially in the second half of
the night. If that happens anyway, it is a good moment for a short rehearsal,
because you may have just come out of a dream, and you are likely to dream again
soon after falling back asleep.

This runbook is for **unplanned** awakenings only.

## Do not

- Do **not** set an alarm to create this awakening. Version 0.1.0 of the
  protocol does not use alarms to interrupt sleep. Alarm-based wake-back-to-bed
  is a recognized technique, but it costs sleep and is out of scope for the first
  experiment.
- Do not get up, turn on bright lights, or look at a bright screen.
- Do not do this if you're having trouble getting back to sleep. Just sleep.
- Do not touch medical alarms. If one woke you, deal with it first; this
  runbook is irrelevant until that is handled.

## Procedure

### 1. Recall (30 seconds)

Lie still. What were you just dreaming? Even a fragment counts. If you remember
anything worth keeping, jot three keywords. Don't write an essay.

### 2. Re-enter (1 minute)

Close your eyes and imagine yourself back in that dream, or any dream if you
can't remember one. Then imagine:

- realizing, in that place, that you have Wi-Fi;
- asking "Am I dreaming?";
- noticing something that doesn't fit;
- recognizing: **"This is a dream."**

Run it once or twice.

### 3. Intention (a few seconds)

> **"When I notice I have Wi-Fi, I'll remember to check whether I'm dreaming."**

### 4. Sleep

Let go of the rehearsal and fall back asleep. If after a few minutes the
rehearsal is keeping you awake, drop it completely.

## No anchor required

You don't need to get up and find the router. The rehearsal works from memory.
Picturing the router briefly is fine; scenario C from
[tonight.md](tonight.md#step-4-practice-three-imagined-scenarios-2-3-minutes),
where there's no router at all, is better.

## Logging

On final waking in the morning, note in the journal that a natural-awakening
rehearsal happened and roughly when, so you can compare nights with and without
it.
