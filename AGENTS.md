# Codex Agent Instructions — D-VISTA

Project: **D-VISTA — Dream Virtual Interface for State Transition and Awareness**. dDNS is a named subsystem.

## Operating constraints

- Treat D-VISTA as a serious, exploratory lucid-dream protocol built on prospective memory, dream incubation, recall, and eventual independent sleep-laboratory validation.
- Maintain a **flat root-level repository**. Every published artifact resides in the root; use descriptive `RFC-*`, `RUNBOOK-*`, and research filenames.
- Treat the core cue as **awareness that Wi-Fi is available**, not a specific router or SSID. The WRT54GS is a waking memory anchor; no physical transmission into dreams is claimed.
- Do not turn hypothesis into verified outcome. Separate subjective recall, external laboratory measurement, and literature evidence. Distinguish N1 creative capture from lucid recognition.
- Never fabricate participants, sessions, statistics, wearable traces, or EEG findings. [OBSERVATIONS.csv](OBSERVATIONS.csv) is intentionally header-only.
- Cite primary research where factual efficacy claims are made; see [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) and [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).
- Preserve ordinary sleep, medical alarms, and waking orientation. No hazardous object-drop implementations or sleep restriction.
- Avoid humor, dismissive language, or sensational claims in published documentation.
- Maintain Markdown-first, zero-runtime-dependency delivery. A CSV observation template is permissible.
- Review [STUDY.md](STUDY.md), [SPEC.md](SPEC.md), [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md), and [DATA-DICTIONARY.md](DATA-DICTIONARY.md) before major edits.
- Verify all relative links and evidence references. Only push, merge, or publish with the owner's permission.
