# D-VISTA: Technical Questions

## Does D-VISTA execute while I am asleep?

The operator rehearses a conditional intention while awake. During subsequent dreams, that memory may become available and lead to explicit recognition of dreaming. “Execution” describes that possible internal sequence; the repository has no software process running inside sleep, no machine listening to dream thoughts, and no actual Wi-Fi bridge between physical and dream environments.

## How can something that operates during sleep be tested?

There are multiple observable outcomes: immediate reports after waking, systematic changes in dream content, prospective-memory cue recognition, and, under appropriate laboratory conditions, time-coded eye/muscle signals from people objectively verified to be in REM sleep. Konkoly et al. (2021) demonstrated real-time exchanges with lucid REM sleepers, [DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026). D-VISTA has not yet demonstrated any of those outcomes in its own study.

## Can failure to induce a lucid dream disprove the entire idea?

A single failed night cannot show the protocol is impossible. Across a defined set of nights, however, **predictions can fail**: the cue might never appear, it might appear without triggering awareness, or sleep might worsen. Such findings would constrain support for the stated hypotheses and guide revisions.

## Why is Wi-Fi the cue?

It is a frequently understood **capability** that may be present as contextual knowledge without requiring a precise visual object. This is an engineering hypothesis, not a verified superiority over other dream signs. [CUE-TAXONOMY.md](CUE-TAXONOMY.md) defines its observable forms.

## Does the WRT54GS need to be transmitting?

No. It is a memory anchor during waking rehearsal. Wi-Fi radio emissions cannot deliver GitHub instructions into dreams.

## Is Dalí/Edison N1 capture the same as lucid dreaming?

No. N1 capture attempts to retrieve images or insights near sleep onset. The Wi-Fi/LCP protocol aims for **recognition while dreaming**. Evidence for creative insight in N1 does not establish lucidity. See [RFC-0007-SLEEP-ONSET-CAPTURE.md](RFC-0007-SLEEP-ONSET-CAPTURE.md).

## Does the protocol require interrupted sleep, substances, or changed medical routines?

No. Adequate, ordinary sleep and all medical alarms take priority. If the exercise worsens sleep, orientation, or anxiety, discontinue it.

## Are any intervention results published?

Not yet. [OBSERVATIONS.csv](OBSERVATIONS.csv) has column names and no observations. See [STUDY.md](STUDY.md) for the prospective measurement plan.
