# Measurement and Interpretation

## Observation layers

| Layer | Instrument | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Waking installation | Operator checklist | The semantic rule was rehearsed | Memory persisted into sleep |
| Subjective dream report | Morning notes | Recalled experience | Objective sleep stage |
| Self-reported lucidity | Report of in-dream recognition | Possible lucid dream | Independent verification of that event |
| Consumer wearable | Device-specific estimation | Approximate sleep timing and possible correlations | Definitive EEG-based REM/N1 or lucidity |
| Laboratory PSG + agreed signaling | EEG/EOG/EMG with precommitted eye movement / muscular response | Objective evidence of a conscious response from verified sleep when protocol criteria are met | Literal internet access during dreaming |

The system operates while the operator is asleep, but this **does not make observations impossible**: published laboratories have recorded time-coded eye signals and even simple dialogue from lucid REM sleepers (Konkoly et al., 2021; DOI [10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026)).

## Primary endpoint

An **E3** is supported when the operator remembers explicitly recognizing the sleep state *before the dream ended*. A dream narrative that includes the words “I am lucid” without evidence of actual awareness is ambiguous.

**The cue and the awareness are independent variables.** Never collapse the following into a single “success”: seeing a WRT54GS, knowing Wi-Fi exists, asking if one is dreaming, recognizing one is dreaming, and performing a chosen dream action.

## Event chronology

For a dream with cue and lucidity, log the sequence:

1. First Wi-Fi or connectivity awareness.
2. Whether the operator remembered the waking intention.
3. Whether a reality-oriented question occurred.
4. First explicit recognition that this is a dream.
5. Action attempted, if any.
6. Wake-up and report time.

If the order cannot be remembered, use `undetermined`, not a reconstructed sequence.

## Quality control

- Score the first immediate account before reading older logs.
- Retain contradictions rather than harmonizing them.
- Compare results by nights, not by sheer number of reported fragments.
- When possible, have a second reader rate anonymized reports **without knowledge of baseline/training labels**. Compute agreement and document disagreements; no need for a complex model in a small sample.
- Published dream quotes are sensitive personal material; require express consent.
- Distinguish “N1-like hypnagogia” from PSG-confirmed N1; a falling key is not a sleep-stage detector.

## Explicit tests that can fail

- Repeated training shows no increase in Wi-Fi incorporation relative to baseline.
- Wi-Fi incorporation increases but **does not** precede lucidity.
- Lucidity occurs independently of Wi-Fi, implying non-specific practice effects.
- Poor recall makes either effect unresolvable.
- Sleep quality deteriorates; the procedure is not acceptable even if cue reports rise.

The hypothesis remains **untested** until actual data are recorded. Absence of positive results over a finite period is not a proof of universal impossibility, but neither is it evidence of success.
